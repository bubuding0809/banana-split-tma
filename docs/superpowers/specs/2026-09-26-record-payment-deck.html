Record Payment

Pay any amount to (or from) another member

Design spec · 2026-09-26 · banana-split-tma

approved design UI-heavy no schema change

Problem

You can only settle the exact debt the app computed. The one-tap "Settled ✅" sends amount: absAmountOwed.

Partial payment

"I owe Alice $50, here's $20 for now." impossible

No debt

"I paid Bob $30 upfront for tickets." impossible
createSettlement already accepts any amount, any direction and any currency. The gap is UI only.
